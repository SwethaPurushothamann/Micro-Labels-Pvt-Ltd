ABOUT MICRO LABELS

Fourteen years of precision, one industrial estate.

Micro Labels Pvt Ltd is a premier manufacturing company based in Bengaluru, specialising in the screen printing of packing cards for precious metals, speedometer dials, decals, overlays, dashboard appliqués and labels. Our facility serves gold refiners & minters and automotive clients across the world, with the machinery and workforce to hold tight tolerance at volume.
